About the Platform

Courage Library is an online educational platform designed to help aspirants prepare for Indian government competitive examinations including SSC, Railway Recruitment Board (RRB), Banking (IBPS, SBI, RBI), Defence (NDA, CDS), and other government recruitment examinations.

Our platform provides the following services:

  • Mock Test Engine: Timed, pattern-based mock examinations with real exam simulation, negative marking, sectional navigation, and leaderboards.
  • Question Bank: A curated library of 8,000+ practice questions including Previous Year Questions (PYQ) across multiple subjects and difficulty levels.
  • Performance Analytics: Detailed analysis of accuracy, speed, subject-wise performance, rank comparison, and improvement tracking.
  • Self-Paced Courses: Structured study material and lesson content aligned with official exam syllabi.
  • Physical Learning Centre: An offline study facility at our Ramaipur, Kanpur location for disciplined in-person study sessions.
Free Access: Mock tests and the question bank are currently available free of charge to all registered users as part of our launch offer. Course content may require enrollment.
