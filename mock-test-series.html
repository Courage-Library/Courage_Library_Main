Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this platform really free? No hidden charges?
Completely free. No registration fee, no monthly subscription, no premium tier. Daily tests, leaderboard, CL Coins, result analysis — not a single rupee. Ever. That's our promise.
Can I attempt more than one test per day?
No — one test per day, and it's only available between 5:00 AM and 11:59 PM. After midnight the test locks and the next one opens at 5 AM. This mirrors real exam discipline — one chance, one day. A missed test is gone for good.
What subjects are covered? What's the weekly schedule?
Each series has its own schedule. For SSC GD: Monday – General Awareness, Tuesday – Reasoning, Wednesday – Quant, Thursday – English, Friday – Hindi, Saturday – Mixed (all 5 subjects), Sunday – Full Mock (100 questions, 60 min). Click "View Schedule" on any series card to see the full plan.
How should I approach negative marking?
Every wrong answer deducts −0.5 marks, exactly matching the SSC and UP Police exam pattern. If you're not confident about an answer, skip it — a blank is better than a wrong attempt. Daily practice sharpens this judgment over time.
How do I earn CL Coins and redeem them?
Every submitted test earns coins — Base coins + Accuracy Bonus + Streak Bonus (the longer your streak, the higher the multiplier — up to ×3.0 at a 50-day streak). Redeem at 1,800 coins for a Steel Bottle, 2,600 for a Diary, or 3,500 for a CL T-Shirt. Physical delivery to your door. Track your balance on the Wallet page.
Which exams are currently available?
Currently live: SSC GD Constable, SSC CGL, and UP Police Constable. Railway (RRB NTPC, Group D), Banking (IBPS PO, Clerk), and Defence (NDA) series are coming soon. Enroll now and you'll get notified when they launch.
Are questions available in Hindi? Is it suitable for Hindi-medium students?
Yes — the platform is fully bilingual. Reasoning, GK, and Quant are available in Hindi. The English section remains in English. The UP Police series is Hindi-first. For some exams you can also choose your preferred language (Hindi or English medium) at the start of the test.
What if my browser crashes or internet cuts out mid-exam?
Your attempt is saved automatically. Go back to your dashboard and you'll see a "Resume Exam" button. However, if too much time has elapsed beyond the exam duration, the attempt is marked as abandoned and that day's test is lost. We recommend a stable connection before starting.
What do I get after submitting a test?
You get your score plus a full breakdown — subject-wise performance, correct/wrong/skipped count, percentile rank, time per question, leaderboard position, and your CL Coins update. The analysis is where real improvement happens, not just the score.
