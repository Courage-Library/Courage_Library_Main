Test Instructions

Please read all instructions carefully before starting the test session.

General Guidelines

  • This test is designed to simulate real competitive exam conditions.
  • The timer will begin immediately after clicking Begin Test Session.
  • Once the timer starts, it cannot be paused.
  • The test will automatically submit when the time limit expires.

Navigation

  • You can move between questions using the Next and Previous buttons.
  • You may change your answers anytime before submitting the test.
  • Use Mark for Review to revisit important questions later.

Question Palette Guide

  • Gray – Question not visited.
  • Yellow – Question visited but not answered.
  • Green – Question answered.
  • Purple – Question marked for review.

Evaluation Policy

  • Each question carries equal marks unless specified otherwise.
  • Unanswered questions receive zero marks.
  • Negative marking (if applicable) will follow the official exam pattern.

Exam Environment Rules

  • The exam must be taken in full-screen mode.
  • Switching tabs or leaving the exam window is not allowed.
  • Developer tools and browser inspection are strictly prohibited.
  • Repeated violations may lead to automatic submission of the test.

Submission

  • You may submit the test anytime using the Submit Test button.
  • The system will automatically submit the test when the timer reaches zero.
  • Once submitted, answers cannot be modified.
Important: The exam session is monitored by the system. Any suspicious activity such as tab switching, exiting full-screen mode, or attempting to inspect the page may result in automatic submission of the test.
