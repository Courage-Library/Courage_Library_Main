Common Questions

Frequently asked about this series

What is the UP Police Constable exam pattern?
UP Police Constable written exam has 150 questions across 4 subjects — General Hindi (37 marks), General Knowledge (38 marks), Numerical & Mental Ability (25 marks), Mental Aptitude/IQ/Reasoning (50 marks). Total 150 marks, 120 minutes. No negative marking.
Is this series available in Hindi?
Yes — this series is Hindi-first. Hindi, GK, Reasoning sections are fully available in Hindi. Numerical Ability has both Hindi and English. The platform is designed for Hindi-medium aspirants across UP.
Is this series free?
Completely free — every test, every analysis, every leaderboard rank. No subscription, no trial period, no premium tier. Free forever.
When is UP Police Bharti 2025 expected?
UP Police Bharti cycles are announced by UPPRPB (Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board). The current bharti cycle is active. Start now — consistent daily practice from today will put you ahead of aspirants who start last-minute.
What if my internet cuts out during a test?
Your attempt is saved automatically as you progress. Go back to your dashboard and you will see a Resume Exam option. However, if the exam window closes (midnight), the attempt is marked as submitted with whatever was answered until that point.
