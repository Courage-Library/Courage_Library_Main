Common Questions

Frequently asked about this series

What posts can I get through SSC CGL?
SSC CGL recruitment covers posts like Income Tax Inspector, Assistant Section Officer, Sub-Inspector CBI, Junior Statistical Officer, and many more across Group B and Group C central government positions.
Does this cover Tier I only or Tier II also?
Currently this series focuses on Tier I — Quantitative Aptitude, General Intelligence, English Comprehension, and General Awareness. Tier II series will be added once Tier I notification is live.
Is this series free?
Completely free — every test, every analysis, every leaderboard rank. No subscription, no trial period, no premium tier. Free forever.
What is the Tier I exam pattern?
SSC CGL Tier I has 100 questions (25 per subject), 200 marks, 60 minutes. Each correct answer is +2 marks, each wrong answer is −0.5. Our full Sunday mock mirrors this exactly.
When is SSC CGL Tier I notification expected?
SSC CGL notifications are released annually by the Staff Selection Commission. The Tier I notification is expected soon based on the current cycle. Start now — the leaderboard is small and your rank matters.
