Common Questions

Frequently asked about this series

What is the Agniveer GD exam pattern?
Agniveer GD written exam (CEE) has 50 questions for 100 marks — General Knowledge (15Q/30M), General Science (15Q/30M), Maths (15Q/30M), and Reasoning (5Q/10M). Duration is 60 minutes. Questions are mostly Class 10 level. -0.5 negative marking.
Is this series available in Hindi?
Yes — this series is fully bilingual. All sections are available in both Hindi and English. You can choose your preferred language when starting any exam on the platform.
Is this series free?
Completely free — every test, every analysis, every leaderboard rank. No subscription, no trial period, no premium tier. Free forever.
What is the Agnipath Scheme?
Agnipath is the Indian government's scheme for short-term military service. Candidates serve 4 years as Agniveers. After 4 years, up to 25% get permanent absorption based on performance. Salary starts at ~₹30,000/month with yearly increments.
What if my internet cuts out during a test?
Your attempt is saved automatically as you progress. Go back to your dashboard and you will see a Resume Exam option. However, if the exam window closes (midnight), the attempt is marked as submitted with whatever was answered until that point.
